## Approved watch platform alternative

Android schedules periods of at least fifteen minutes through WorkManager. Faster watches use a foreground service and alarm-backed ticker. See `watch/WatchScheduler.kt`.

Approved iOS behavior: foreground fast checks, discretionary background checks through BGProcessingTask, due reminders through local notifications and catch-up on reopen. Show the requested cadence and actual last-run time. Never display a guaranteed next inference time while iOS controls scheduling. On iOS 26, foreground-started work can use BGContinuedProcessingTask, which does not supply an unattended periodic service. JC approved this alternative on 2026-10-02. Its management, model-check, timer/background and notification code is integrated. State/tool/controller checks and a real CPU watch pass. Discretionary OS background behavior, notifications and native navigation remain unverified.
